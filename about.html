Who we are

Local Samui family · Koh Samui operators

Yui Villas is a small rental brand from a family who lives and works on the island.

Homes looked after by people who know Samui

We’re a local Koh Samui family offering furnished villas for guests who want a comfortable, private place to stay — without the friction of a big agency.

Our first listing is a bright 2-bedroom villa on Bonkai Road, behind the 7-Eleven: fully furnished, spacious, and ready for short or longer stays. As inventory grows, each home will get its own clear page with photos, facts and direct contact.

You deal with us directly on WhatsApp or phone. Ask about dates, what’s included, and we’ll confirm availability.
